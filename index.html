What We Make

Containers your customers pick up and trust.

Our Heritage

Purpose. Precision. Performance.

In 1994, A.W. Barghout & son M.B. Barghout co-founded Barplas Ltd in Bradford, England — becoming the second largest manufacturer of plastic containers in the UK. After the sale of Barplas UK to RPC Group plc in 2006, the family brought that same expertise to Lebanon. Barplas Lebanon is today owned and led by Mohamad Abdul Hafiz Barghout.
