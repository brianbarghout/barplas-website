Decoration Technologies

Three Ways to Make
Your Container Stand Out

01

IML — In-Mould Labelling

The label is placed inside the mould before injection, becoming a permanent, seamless part of the container wall. The result is photographic-quality decoration that cannot peel, fade or scratch — ideal for premium brands and shelf-ready packaging.

02

Dry Offset Printing

Using Desco equipment — the world standard for pail and bucket decoration — we print directly onto the finished container in up to six colours in a single pass, including full-process and half-tone artwork. Fast, consistent, and highly cost-effective for volume runs.

03

Heat Transfer Printing

High-definition decoration applied to finished containers, offering maximum design flexibility and rich colour reproduction. Well-suited to complex artwork, gradients and detailed brand graphics.
